Page: `/ma-soi` (open to all staff). API: `/api/ma-soi`. No env vars, no cron.

3. After deploy, log in as dev → `/ma-soi` → "Mở ván mới". Others open `/ma-soi` and join. Dev presses start (or auto-start at N players).

## Rules built
- Roles: Sói, Dân, Tiên tri, Bảo vệ, Phù thủy, Thợ săn. Roles are never sent to other players' browsers.
- One poll per phase, matched to the 24h clock: night ends when everyone has answered or at 00:00; day vote ends when everyone has voted or at 19:00 (Vietnam time).
- No cron: the clock is stored (phase_ends_at) and resolved lazily the next time anyone loads the page or acts.
- Tie → new poll with only the tied people. Wolves vote, and the kill counts after everyone confirms.
- 2 phases missed in a row = out. Day discussion is in person or in chat.
- Page polls every 20s.
