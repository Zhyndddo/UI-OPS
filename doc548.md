# Round 548 — corner section switcher + compact Non-Package Booking filters (tester account only)

Request: a button under the topbar, right corner, labelled like the sidebar item; click → turns once, then the other workstation / ticket types slide in from the right as a vertical stack of banners (current one left out); page numbers to the left of the button when the stack does not fit; a dash button for a numbered quick list when there are many pages. Plus compact filters for Non-Package Booking. Both applied to the tester's login first, then global.

## Who sees it
Only accounts in `lib/uiTesters.js` → `UI_TESTER_EMAILS = ["an.thien@gmail.com", "an.thien@vieent.vn"]` (matched on the REAL profile, not "view as"). Everyone else sees exactly the old pills and the old filter rows. (The vieent.vn address is the one the app already knows as Zhyn's account; both are listed so it works whichever email the profile row carries.)

## Corner switcher (lib/SectionSwitcher.js, mounted in lib/AppShell.js)
- Shows on Workstation pages (/workstation/*, /booking) labelled "Workstation", and on Tickets pages (/tickets/*) labelled "Tickets". Other sidebar items have nothing to switch, so nothing shows there.
- Click: button turns once (Y-axis), banners slide in from the right, staggered. Click it again, click outside, press Esc, or pick a banner: banners slide out and the button turns once more.
- Vertical stack; the page you are on is left out; last banner is "All Workstations / All Tickets" (the old "← All …" button). Fixed order = the old pills' order. Counts load the first time it opens and are reused for a minute.
- Page size is measured from the window height (min 3 banners). More than one page → page numbers left of the button. More than 3 pages → a "—" button: numbered quick list of every banner.
- Old TypeSwitcher pills are hidden for the tester (counts are not fetched for them either).
- Respects "reduce motion". Mobile: same, 8px from the edge.
- Heads-up: it floats over the top-right corner of the page, so it can sit on top of a page's own right-aligned buttons in that spot.

## Non-Package Booking filters (lib/NpbFilterBar.js, used in app/workstation/non-package-booking/page.js)
One row: search, a "Filters" button showing the path (e.g. TikTok Channel › Partner › BK MUSIC) that opens a small panel (Category / Group / Brand), the Unassigned chip with its count, and Reset. Picking a brand closes the panel. Page state and filtering are unchanged; the last category / group / brand is remembered in this browser (`npb_filters_v1`).
