# Round 559 (v2) — Halloween feedback + chat: DMs, private channels, subscribe

1. Spiderweb is back, but as a small web in the top-RIGHT corner of the screen (edges run along the screen edges, so no stray slash).
2. Pumpkin face redrawn: eyes sit in the side lobes, small nose in the centre, wide toothy mouth across all three lobes.
3. Pumpkin is clickable: click = light on/off. 30 clicks = it splits in half, candles + wax + a tiny note appear.
   Click the note -> "Chúc mừng bạn đã tìm được phần thưởng là 1 cây kem thật ngon lành" popup, and a bell notification
   ("Ice cream owed") goes to an.thien@vieent.vn with the finder's name. Row saved in halloween_finds (one per person).
   After that the person can still break the pumpkin, but no note appears. Not clicking the note: pumpkin re-forms after 25 s.
4. Chat bubble gets an orange ring during Halloween.

## Chat (everyone, not just tester)
- Direct messages: "+ New message" -> pick a person. Readable only by the two people (database-enforced; dev/admin cannot read them). Always notify.
- Subscribe button (top right of every channel): number on the bubble + sound only for subscribed channels and DMs. @mention in an unsubscribed channel does NOT ping (say if you want mentions to always ping).
- Admin/dev: "+ New channel" (hidden from everyone but creator + you until people are added), delete channel (🗑), 👥 People (add/remove). People added to a private channel start subscribed (they can unsubscribe).
- Dev (you): sees private channel NAMES (greyed, 🔒) and its people list, never the messages. Adding yourself as a member is visible in the list.
- Existing channels stay open to everyone, start unsubscribed. Members: cannot create/delete/add (checked in the DB, not just the screen).
- 559c: top line now starts at the sidebar edge; bottom bar glows while the pumpkin is lit (off when dark/stumps); after the first break the pumpkin cycles lit -> dark -> candle stumps -> lit on click (saved on this device, no auto switch back); note now flies out of the pumpkin.
- Follow-up: fix-round559-chat-tighten-grants.sql (only if the main SQL was already run) removes default TRUNCATE/DELETE grants.
- Files: lib/ChatDock.js, lib/chatProto.js, lib/chatGuides.js (legend text), lib/patchNotes.js, sql/pending/add-round559-chat-private-dm-subscribe.sql
